# Apple Crisp

This [Easy Apple Crisp With Oat Topping](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/229088/apple-crisp-with-oat-topping/) by realnakedchef 
is a staple at our Christmas and Thanksgiving get-togethers. Assemble the raw ingredients, wrap them in plastic, and it travels well!

- Toss
 - 5 apples: peeled, cored, and sliced
 - 2 T sugar
 - 1/2 t cinnamon

- Spread in a 9" square pan

- Cut/cream together
 - 1 c brown sugar
 - 3/4 c oats
 - 3/4 c flour
 - 1 t cinnamon
 - 1/2 c butter

- Sprinkle oat mixture evenly over apples.

- Bake 40 minutes at 350 degrees.

# Spiced Brownies

Also from J's mother, originally. This recipe cooks faster than normal brownies, and you can do all the melting and stirring in a 2c pyrex 
measuring cup if you're careful.

- Melt together
 - 1/4 c butter
 - 2 oz unsweetened chocolate

- Stir in
 - 1 c white sugar
 - 3/8 t cinnamon
 - 2 pinches cayenne

- Stir in
 - 2 eggs
 - 10 drops orange extract

- Stir in
 - 1/2 c flour
 - 1/4 t salt

- Bake in a 9" square pan for 15 minutes at 350 degrees.

# Brazilian Carrot Cake

The flavor profile is foreign, but this is a great way to use up excess carrots! Based on the recipe of the same name at [Olivia's Cuisine](https://www.oliviascuisine.com/brazilian-carrot-cake/),

- Blend on low speed
 - 4 large carrots, cut in chunks
 - 3 large eggs
 - 1 c vegetable oil

- Stir in
 - 1 c sugar

- Gently add then stir in
 - 2 c flour
 - Pinch of salt
 - 1 T baking powder

- Pour into two buttered loaf pans and bake at 350 for 50 minutes or until a toothpick inserted comes out clean.

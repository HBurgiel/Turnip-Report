# Squash, Onion, Butter, and Sage
Based on [Savory Bread Pudding with Roasted Butternut Squash, Onion, and Sage](http://www.lemonythyme.com/savory-bread-pudding-with-roasted-butternut-squash-onion-sage/) by Libby with Lemony Thyme.

(Sage optional)

- Dice
  - small butternut squash
- Toss with  
  - small, thinly sliced onion
  - 3 cloves chopped garlic
- Stir in
  - 1/2 t salt
  - 1/2 t thyme
  - 1/4 t black pepper
- Add
  - 1/2 stick butter
  - 1/4 c milk
- Microwave 5 minutes on high
- Stir in
  - 3 eggs
  - 3/4 c milk
  - 3/4 c grated Parmesan

- Cube
  - 8 c bread

- Toss together in large bowl
- Let sit 15 minutes in large casserole dish
- Bake 40-45 minutes at 400 until a knife inserted comes out clean

# Cheesy Zucchini Casserole

Based on [the Allrecipes recipe](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/20867/cheesy-zucchini-casserole-i/) by DC1.

- Toss
 - 4 slices bread, cubed
 - 1/4 c. melted butter

- Add
 - 2 c cubed zucchini
 - 1 large onion, chopped

- Beat together
 - 1 egg
 - 1 t garlic salt

- Stir both mixtures together with
 - 2 c grated cheddar

- Bake in a covered dish at 350 degrees for 30 minutes. Remove the cover and bake for another 30 minutes or until a knife inserted comes out clean.
